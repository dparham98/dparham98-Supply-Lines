# Grenadier modelling prompt

Reference: `Blender/References/Grenadier_concept.webp`.

Build a game-ready, low-poly character for an RTS seen from a high camera: a tall, athletic woman, a cyborg explosives specialist, standing in a relaxed A-pose. About 1.8 m to the top of the cap, facing -Y, Z up, feet on the ground at Z = 0. Budget about 25,000 triangles. Flat PBR materials with no textures, matching the Scout and tank. Separate named meshes so she can be rigged later without re-cutting her.

**Silhouette, in order of importance.** It must read at game distance: (1) long officer's greatcoat, fitted at the waist, flaring to the lower shin, open in an inverted V at the front; (2) wide-crowned peaked cap; (3) broad armoured shoulders; (4) backpack of canisters behind the shoulders; (5) grenade launcher with a fat drum, carried low at her right side, muzzle down.

**Palette.** Charcoal coat and tunic, gunmetal armour, oxblood red trim, tan-brown leather, brass fittings, amber-orange bands on the grenades, blonde hair. The only glow is the amber panel on the backpack.

**Head.** Strong, angular female face with a defined jaw, grey-green eyes and pale skin. A steel cyborg plate along her right cheek and jaw. Blonde hair drawn back into stacked buns at the nape. Black peaked cap with red piping and a glossy visor, the crown rising at the front. Brass-rimmed goggles resting on the cap above the visor, on a leather strap.

**Torso.** High black collar with red edging. Dark tunic. Layered chest and collar plates with red trim. Webbing over the chest, a row of five grenades across the stomach. A wide leather belt with a brass buckle, leather pouches on both hips, more grenades at her left hip.

**Coat.** Heavy charcoal skirts, red along the front edges and hem, red lining showing inside the opening, a split at the back, red piping on the rear panels. A plated tabard hanging at the front between the coat's edges.

**Limbs.** Everything from the shoulder down is machine. Large rounded shoulder plates with red trim. Steel upper arms and forearm housings with exposed elbow joints and ribbed wrists, articulated steel hands. Legs in dark bodysuit with faceted knee plates, long shin guards and chunky armoured boots with heavy soles, straps and buckles.

**Backpack.** A central block with an amber grid panel in the middle of its back face, two upright canisters each side, a cross canister on top, brass pipes, leather straps over the shoulders.

**Grenade launcher, a separate object.** Skeletal stock, angular receiver, pistol grip, fat canister drum under the receiver with its axis along the barrel, short thick barrel with a heavy muzzle sleeve, top rail and sight, red accents on the receiver.

**Grenade, a separate object for later use as a projectile.** Steel canister with a neck, cap and spoon lever, one amber band.

**Do not include** any real-world insignia, flags or symbols. The crosses on the concept's arm, shoulder and cap, and the cap badge, become plain armour and cloth.
