# Supply Lines Blender projects

This is the main location for Supply Lines Blender work. Open these for future edits:

- Characters\Scout\Scout.blend: the 2026-11-10 rigged skater scout, reworked to References\Scout_concept.webp from `PROMPT.md` beside it (armature `Skater rig`, 39 bones, actions idle/run/shoot/die), see docs/SCOUT_MODEL.md. Two heads sit on the `head` bone: `head_helmet`, and `head_bare` with `head_bare_hair` and `head_bare_goggles`; the game shows one per Scout. Skinned meshes are `body_suit`, `armor`, `gear`, `hands`, `skates` and `ponytail`; `shotgun`, `shotgun_pump` and `skate_wheel_L1..R4` are parented to bones. Keep `Muzzle flash`, `Muzzle light` and `Shell casing` and their bones, and do not name a mesh after a bone. The concept's crosses are replaced by the faction fang (`faction_fang` from the Drone) on both shoulder plates. Flat PBR materials, except `scout_head_basecolor` (face, hair and ponytail) and `scout_helmet_basecolor`, both packed. The scripts that built it are in Characters\Scout\build; the revision before the rework is Backups\Scout\Scout_2026-11-10_before_rework.blend. Exported to assets\models\units\scout.glb with subdivision off and animations as actions. Collection `scout_death_fx` (switched off in the view layer) holds the oil, sparks and wiring for `die`, baked per object as NLA tracks named Death and exported to assets\models\effects\scout_death_oil.glb; every object is keyed to the body, so rebuild it whenever `die` changes.
- Characters\Infantry\Infantry.blend: the 2026-11-08 rigged cyborg infantry (armature `infantry_rig2`, 22 bones, actions Rest/Run/Hack/Shoot on NLA tracks; the `rig_artpose` collection is what ships, `rigprep_Apose` and `infantry_hunyuan_raw` are working references). Exported to assets\models\units\infantry.glb: select the `rig_artpose` objects, glTF Binary, +Y up, apply modifiers, animations as NLA tracks, lights on. The game plays the clips by name and fires from the `flash` bone at the barrel tip, whose scale (keyed near zero in every clip) drives `Muzzle flash` and `Muzzle light`; the shot lands on frame 3 of Shoot. Collection `infantry_projectile` holds `infantry_bullet` and its child `infantry_bullet_trail` (forward -Y; move the parent to the origin to export both to assets\models\projectiles\infantry_bullet.glb, no animations or lights; the game colours a mesh with `trail` in its name by team); `chainsaw_teeth_rig` must stay a separate mesh under the `chainsaw` bone for the teeth shader. `Die` is the death clip; collection `infantry_death_fx` (switched off in the view layer) holds its oil, sparks and wiring, baked per object as NLA tracks named Death and exported to assets\models\effects\infantry_death_oil.glb, so rebuild it whenever `Die` changes. The earlier static infantry is Backups\Infantry\Infantry_2026-10-15_static.blend.
- Characters\Grenadier\Grenadier.blend: the 2026-11-09 Grenadier, modelled from `PROMPT.md` beside it and References\Grenadier_concept.webp, with the concept's crosses and cap badge left off. Rigged and animated (Rest, Run, Shoot, Die), exported to assets/models/units/grenadier.glb, see docs/GRENADIER_MODEL.md; the unrigged revision is in Backups\Grenadier. Collection `grenadier` holds `grenadier_rig`, one named mesh per body region plus `grenade_launcher`; collection `grenadier_grenade` (switched off for review renders) holds the loose `grenade`.
- Vehicles\Maglev\MaglevTracks.blend and MaglevTrain.blend: the 2026-11-08 maglev set at true scale (collection `maglev_tracks`: `track_straight` 8 m with `track_straight_A/B` end markers, `track_curve` radius 20 m quarter turn with `track_curve_A/B`; collection `maglev_train`: `train_engine_root` and `train_cargo_root` with bodies, two sleds each, couplers and the cargo indicator). Exported per root object to assets\models\rails\maglev_track_*.glb and assets\models\units\maglev_train_*.glb (the root moved to the origin for export; glTF Binary, +Y up, apply modifiers). Placed in the game at 0.6; the game relies on the end markers' positions, the sled and coupler names and the curve bending toward local +X.
- Vehicles\CargoTruck\CargoTruck.blend: the 2026-11-08 cargo truck (collection `cargo_truck`: `truck_root` > `chassis` with `cab`, `cargo_bed` > `cargo_lid` > `cargo_indicator`, `rear_ramp`, `exhaust_stack`, `steer_FL/FR` empties holding `wheel_FL/FR`, and `wheel_ML/MR/RL/RR`; eight untextured materials, `team_color` surfaces on cab, bed and lid). No baked actions: the game articulates the parts itself (wheels roll on X, steer pivots turn on Y, lid opens +60 deg and ramp -125 deg on X, indicator rises 0.32), so keep those names, pivots and axes. Exported to assets\models\units\cargo_truck.glb: select the collection's meshes and empties, glTF Binary, +Y up, apply modifiers, no lights or cameras.
- Vehicles\Drone\Drone.blend: the 2026-11-09 Drone, modelled from References\Drone_concept.webp in its base form (the Recon, Support and Cargo variants are not built), with the concept's cross replaced by the faction fang from the cargo truck (`faction_fang`). Animated (Rest, Run, Die as per-object NLA tracks) and exported to assets/models/units/drone.glb, see docs/DRONE_MODEL.md; the unanimated revision is in Backups\Drone. Collection `drone` holds `drone_root` > `hull`, `comms`, `antennas`, `arms`, `faction_fang`, `sensor` and `duct_FL/FR/RL/RR`, each duct holding its `rotor_XX` on the hub. Faces -Y, Z up, lowest point on Z = 0 at rest. Collection `drone_death_fx` (switched off in the view layer) holds the crash, baked per object as NLA tracks named Death on Die's timeline and exported to assets/models/effects/drone_death_fx.glb, so rebuild it whenever `Die` changes.
- Vehicles\Tank\Tank.blend: the 2026-11-08 tank (hull, turret and barrel under Tank_Root in the Tank collection; baked PBR textures packed). Exported to assets\models\units\tank.glb. The game turns `Tank_Turret` about its own origin (keep it on the ring centre) and fires from `Barrel_Muzzle_Brake`; keep those object names and the barrel parented under the turret. The firing effects are the `tank_fire_fx`, `tank_shell` and `tank_impact` collections (switched off in the view layer); they export on their own to assets\models\projectiles. Do not re-export tank.glb for them, because the track shader depends on its vertex order. The earlier unvalidated tank is Backups\Tank\Tank_2025-09-13_unvalidated.blend. Collections `tank_death_cookoff`, `tank_death_bleed` and `tank_death_overload` (switched off in the view layer) hold the three deaths, exported to assets\models\effects	ank_death_*.glb. Their objects carry a clip prefix in the .blend, which comes off for export so each GLB has plain node names.

References holds recovered art. Previews\Scout holds current renders. Backups\Scout holds earlier revisions. Archive\Originals preserves the old Supply Lines art folder, including Blender backups, exports, animations and related assets.

Keep editing the same named working file per unit. Use Backups for revisions. Do not edit archived originals. Older Codex and OneDrive copies remain preserved; use these local working files going forward.

.gdignore prevents Godot from importing this work-in-progress folder. Put intentional game-ready exports in the game's assets folder separately.

Copy provenance and SHA256 verification are in migration_manifest.json. Scout reference images are packed. Older projects may retain external image paths; use References or Archive copies for relinking if needed.
- Depot: `Blender/Buildings/Depot/Depot.blend` (the `depot_warehouse` collection) exports to `assets/models/buildings/depot.glb`. `Depot_tractor_exit.blend` and `Depot_truck_assemble.blend` are its production clips; see `docs/DEPOT_MODEL.md`.
- Terrain: `Blender/Terrain/GroundTiles.blend` (the `ground_tiles` collection) holds four tiles each of grass, desert, water, forest and mountain (the game does not use the mountains); its textures are in `Blender/Terrain/ground_tiles/textures`. See `docs/GROUND_TILES.md` for rebuilding the texture arrays used in game.
