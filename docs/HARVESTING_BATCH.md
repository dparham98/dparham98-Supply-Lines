# Shared buildable mines and universal forestry

Second economy stage implemented. Latest shared-mine and every-forest-tile instructions supersede the previous ownership/capture mine prototype and marked-stand-only forestry.

## Iron mines

- Infantry and Depots have Iron Mine in their existing Build menu. Construction costs 75 energy / 25 iron from the explicitly chosen local Depot, with existing builder-selection/territory checks. Overlapping territories never pool funds. Default footprint is 2x2, and every footprint cell must be an empty iron-deposit (ORE) tile with some remaining iron across the footprint. Mixed ground, water, forest, other structures, overlapping mines and exhausted footprints are rejected without charging.
- Mines are shared infrastructure, not capturable. Infantry of either team can gather from a living mine. Bare deposits cannot be extracted directly. The player can explicitly Attack a shared mine; incidental auto-targeting/splash does not target neutral mines. Mine health remains the editable 1,000 HP prototype.
- Either side's Infantry can repair a damaged mine, paying its own friendly local stockpile. Until mobile supplies arrive, this means one explicitly supplied or latched nearest covering friendly Depot, with the existing repair range and 10 HP / second for 2 E / 1 I. Repairs cannot resurrect destroyed structures; Depot capture remains unchanged.
- Iron belongs to deposit cells, not mine buildings: default 250 iron per tile, so a fresh 2x2 mine accesses 1,000. Extraction reduces shared per-cell pools atomically. Destruction removes the building and releases its claim without refilling the deposit. Rebuilding—even on a different overlapping set of deposit cells—sees only the remaining iron. A destroyed mine stops extraction immediately; workers need a new gather order after rebuilding. Exhausted mines remain buildings, and exhausted deposit terrain stays blocked; this batch does not clear ore rocks/terrain.
- The starting scene's formerly off-deposit mine was moved onto the existing marked iron deposit. It is a valid authored starter mine so the normal economy can gather iron without a circular requirement to already own iron. The old bare marker is not harvestable and hides underneath a living mine. No new deposit pixels were painted.

Edit mine cost, health and footprint in assets/economy/engineering_rules.tres. Edit per-tile iron and wood quantities in assets/economy/harvesting_rules.tres; restart after resource changes.

## Forestry

- Every existing FOREST tile can now supply 20 wood. Right-click a forest ground cell with Infantry, or use its Gather card and click the cell. Only the targeted cell is harvested; there is no automatic forest-area chaining yet. Dense interior cells may remain unreachable until an edge is cleared, which preserves forest obstruction. Click the ground/base of trees to target the terrain cell.
- Infantry uses its existing range, 10-unit carry capacity and 5-per-0.6-second harvest cycle, returning wood to a friendly Depot. Full storage retains carried wood. Both sides share the same finite forest tile; simultaneous harvesters cannot duplicate it.
- On depletion, exactly that tile becomes open grass and updates navigation, the terrain clearing mask, minimap and existing native forest batch visuals. Tank clearing destroys any remaining wood without crediting it. Previously harvested/carried wood stays carried if another actor clears its source.
- Logic-only harvest handles are created lazily for clicked cells; no tree meshes, UI or terrain are regenerated by gameplay. Existing saved trees represent 2x2 patches, so canopy removal remains coarser than exact-cell navigation/material/resource state. Adjacent uncleared forest cells remain forest even if their shared canopy instance was hidden. Legacy authored wood stands remain additional sources for scenario compatibility, but are no longer the only harvestable wood.
- Resource pools and clearing are match-local. Restart restores original reserves and forest. source_map.png, terrain_data.png, terrain_cells.bin and terrain_landmarks.tscn remain unchanged.

## Editor tests

1. Open scenes/maps/harvesting_exercise.tscn and press F6. It starts with an Infantry and Depot beside existing forest. The inherited hostile is disabled.
2. Select Infantry and right-click a forest ground tile immediately left of it. Watch two loads of wood reach ForestDepot and the depleted tile turn to bare ground. Try the Gather card on another tile.
3. Press F to return to the main Depot, or use the minimap. The existing shared mine sits on the red iron patch left of that Depot. Additional 2x2 patches immediately below it can be mined: select Depot or its Infantry, Build / Iron Mine, then place a green-valid footprint entirely on iron. Invalid grass footprints charge nothing.
4. Select Infantry and right-click a mine to gather. Use its Repair card to restore a damaged mine and its Attack card to explicitly damage one. Selecting the mine shows shared access, health and reserves, with no capture controls. Enemy-side access/repair and rebuild conservation are exercised by the regression test without adding enemy strategy to the scenario.

Coal deposits, mobile supply deployment, dropped piles, generator fuel/water operation and vehicle fuel consumption remain later stages. Wood still has no burning/spending behavior until the generator stage. Broader forest/resource redistribution and map tiling are not part of this change.

Later update: wood/coal generator burning is now implemented; see GENERATOR_BATCH.md. Earlier descriptions of free generator output or wood having no energy use are historical and superseded. Water/vehicle operating fuel and coal extraction remain pending.
