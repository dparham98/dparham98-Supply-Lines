# Bottom HUD and contextual construction

The two side panels are replaced by one bottom HUD in both the F5 skirmish and the F6 supply exercise. Restart the running scene to load the new saved UI. All controls are authored in scenes/ui/skirmish_hud.tscn; scripts update selection, text, visibility and callbacks. No new enemy strategy or Blender model was added. The 500-iron sandbox grant remains enabled.

## What the HUD shows

Bottom left is a north-up minimap of the original source map, with live unit/building/resource markers, a white visible-ground camera outline and selected-object highlights. Blue is player, red hostile, grey neutral, gold resource. Click the minimap to focus the camera without selecting units or issuing movement orders. World X maps left-to-right and world Z top-to-bottom. The map is a full-map overview without fog of war or zoom; clustered actors overlap at this scale. Map pixels remain unchanged. Its bounds are editable on the saved Map control script (default -250 to +250 on both axes).

The middle displays selected object name, health bar/numbers and relevant details. Infantry shows carried iron/capacity, nearest friendly Depot and straight-line distance in world units, speed, vision, attack range, effective damage/interval and production costs. Unresolved resource attacks explicitly label the compatibility fallback; disabled unresolved attacks say unavailable. Cargo shows separate carried energy/iron, combined capacity and current route task. Depot Trucks show deployment starter stock, not transferable cargo. Multiple selected units show a primary unit and additional count; details are for the primary unit, not an aggregated army total. Existing friendly-unit click/drag selection is preserved; Depots (including neutral/enemy) and generators are clickable. Empty space clears building selection. Enemy unit inspection is not added.

The right is the command card. Infantry has Move, Attack, Gather iron, Stop, Return carried iron and Build. Click Move/Attack/Gather, then click an appropriate world target; Esc cancels. Existing right-click orders remain available. Group movement/stop/return apply to selected units; non-combat vehicles reject attack/gather. A Depot has three distinct production buttons: Infantry, Depot Truck, Cargo Vehicle, with local costs. Its details show only that Depot's inventory, generation, queue and capture progress. Neutral/enemy Depots expose information without production commands. A Depot Truck exposes deploy and movement/stop; a cargo vehicle exposes clearly labelled load/unload endpoint menus, per-trip maximum amounts, repeat, assign, pause/resume and cancel. Cargo can still move with right-click; route controls operate on the primary selected cargo vehicle. A generator displays its fixed assigned Depot, generation rate/paused state and Focus assigned Depot.

Focus selection and Restart remain in the HUD header. The header carries the last order/validation message or existing skirmish outcome. The old objective checklist is removed from the UI; its underlying gather/print/kill scenario win logic remains. The HUD continues consuming its clicks, but edge panning bypasses the entire HUD, including the minimap and command-card children. Move the pointer to a screen edge to pan even over the HUD. This uses the existing edge_pan_passthrough metadata and leaves mouse filters/button handling unchanged; arrows work as before. No live pointer behavior is certified by automated tests.

## Infantry construction and local payment

Select infantry, click Build. The menu lists friendly Depots whose construction territory currently contains that infantry. It initially chooses the nearest eligible Depot; the list shows each candidate's own current energy/iron. The selected payer's stock is repeated prominently above the build buttons. Choose a different payer explicitly in overlapping territory. Stocks never pool. If the chosen Depot is lost or the infantry leaves its territory, spending is blocked; another rich nearby Depot is not silently substituted. Choose a new payer explicitly. If no Depot covers the infantry, move into friendly territory first.

Click Depot or Generator, then click valid empty land. The infantry must remain selected, alive, player-owned and within the chosen payer's territory when placement is committed. All new building footprint corners must also remain in that payer's territory, and every footprint cell must be buildable/unoccupied. Existing preview and payment checks run again on commit; invalid actions charge nothing. The generator is assigned only to that payer. The infantry remains selected after construction and retains the payer context. Esc cancels placement; Back closes the build menu. Changing selection cancels the old build context.

This is an explicit extension of the previous instantaneous construction model: no build time, worker travel-to-site or adjacency requirement is introduced. Infantry can place anywhere valid inside its chosen local Depot territory while itself remaining inside that territory. Existing direct construction methods keep an explicit payer API for scenarios/tests, but the player HUD exposes construction through infantry only. Depot production does not expose a build shortcut. No other unit is assumed to be an engineer: can_construct defaults false on the shared controller and is enabled on the gameplay infantry scene. Depot Trucks keep their separate one-time deploy mechanic; cargo vehicles do not build. The seven existing UnitStats resources and ability identifiers are untouched.

## Building health decision

Previous Depots/generators had no health fields. To meet selected-building health display, they now have editable Max Health and per-instance runtime health, provisionally 1000 HP for Depots and 300 for generators. These values are new placeholders, not recovered historical balance. Capture retains existing health. Their take_damage method clamps negative damage and removes the building at zero, releasing footprint claims on exit; generators stop and cargo routes handle removed endpoints as before. Existing enemy targeting still selects units only. There is no new building attack order, repair mechanic, regeneration, armor or enemy pressure in this change.

## Editor checks

1. F5: click the starting Depot; verify its name, HP, 500 test iron, generation, queue and production card. Print cargo or infantry and confirm only its inventory is charged.
2. Select infantry; verify HP comes from infantry.tres (55), cargo, nearest Depot distance and stats. Test click/drag selection, right-click orders and command-card Move/Attack/Gather followed by a world click. Stop and Return should work.
3. Open Build. Check local inventory and payer name. Build a generator on green land; verify local payment, assignment and income. Try occupied ground, outside-territory placement and insufficient energy. Use Back and Esc. Move infantry outside all Depot rings and verify no building can be paid for.
4. Move infantry into overlapping Depot territory, choose the poorer payer, and confirm a rich neighbour cannot fund it. Capture or leave the chosen payer territory while placing; verify it does not switch payer silently.
5. Select a cargo vehicle. Assign a route through the bottom command card, inspect carried quantities/task and exercise pause/resume/cancel. Select a Depot Truck and verify only deployment is offered, not delivery routes or infantry construction.
6. Click a generator to inspect its HP, assigned Depot and rate; click neutral Depot for ownership/capture information without player production. Click empty land to clear selection. Minimap clicks should move the camera only; white outline should follow. Check arrow pan, wheel zoom, Q/E release and world-edge scrolling.
