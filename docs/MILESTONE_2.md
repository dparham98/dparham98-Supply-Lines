# Milestone 2: local Depot economy

Historical milestone snapshot: Milestone 3 adds assigned local generators, explicit cargo routes and placement previews. See MILESTONE_3.md for current testing and supply rules.

Implemented in C:/Dev/Supply-Lines. This supersedes the global Ore payment rules in FIRST_CONTACT.md and RECOVERED_DESIGN.md; those files describe the prior prototype. Unit resources and unresolved combat fallback retain their existing behavior. Ore deposits now deliver iron, one cargo unit to one iron. There is no automatic Depot transfer, shared wallet, energy regeneration, logistics network, save/load, enemy production AI or new building class beyond Depots.

## Explicit provisional decisions

All new values are prototype decisions, not recovered historical balance. Edit assets/economy/depot_rules.tres in Inspector: construction radius 35 world units; capture radius 12; capture time 8 seconds; starting inventory 500 energy / 0 iron; direct construction 200 energy / 40 iron; truck production 150 energy / 30 iron; deployed inventory 100 energy / 0 iron. Directly constructed Depots start empty. Starter energy is finite scenario supply; this milestone adds no energy generator. An empty Depot can accept iron but requires a future energy supply mechanic or scenario-authored energy to print. Unit production uses its assigned UnitStats (currently infantry 100 energy / 20 iron). Printer duration 3 seconds, cap 3, and 8 × 8 Depot footprints remain editable scene properties. Truck reuses the unit controller and an explicitly stretched engineer placeholder: provisional 80 HP, speed 3, no gathering, firing or capture capability. Its cost and starter cargo are configured in DepotRules, not invented as a historical UnitStats entry.

A single living non-truck team's units within capture radius accumulate time regardless of orders. Owner presence or multiple teams resets progress (no numerical advantage). Leaving resets progress. Neutral ownership can be authored with team = "neutral". After 8 uninterrupted seconds, ownership changes immediately, stored resources remain intact, all paid queues are cancelled without refund, and progress resets. Buildings do not fight or take damage; enemy units can capture by proximity but have no new strategic AI. Capture and queues are processed on the Depot. Production only runs for player ownership.

Construction requires selecting a player Depot. All four footprint corners must be inside THAT Depot's radius, and every cell must be empty/buildable. Overlap never pools balances: only the selected Depot pays, even if a neighbour has enough. Failed validation or payment changes nothing. Direct construction is instantaneous; no worker requirement or construction timer. Production is prepaid atomically at the owning printer, checks cap first, and retains payment while an exit is blocked. Spawn uses the existing exit search.

Select one truck and press Deploy selected truck. Deployment stops it, validates the exact local 8 × 8 footprint (ignoring only its own reservation), consumes it, then creates a player Depot outside or inside existing territory with the configured starter inventory. It does not pay a second construction fee. No relocation to distant valid land is allowed. Invalid deployment retains the truck and its reservation; its movement order has stopped. If several trucks are selected, one is deployed per button press. No Depot resource is deducted, transferred or generated each tick.

Right-click a friendly Depot to explicitly choose cargo recipient. Automatic gathering returns to the last friendly recipient, or nearest friendly Depot by straight-line distance when none is valid. If unreachable, the existing path retry waits; it does not teleport cargo or search every alternate reachable Depot. Ownership is checked again before delivery. Iron credits only that recipient. The game.ore compatibility alias refers only to starting Depot iron; it is not a wallet or used for payment. Victory still requires delivery, infantry printing and hostile elimination. Defeat checks all friendly local production inventories and queues.

## Editor test instructions

Open scenes/maps/resource_skirmish.tscn and press F6 (or F5 for the main scene). Left-click Depot: its blue ring shows its construction radius and HUD shows its local balances. Left-click/drag infantry; right-click Ore to gather iron. Click Depot again and print infantry after 20 iron is delivered. Fight the hostile as before. Check Q/E release, arrows, wheel zoom and screen-edge scrolling.

Gather 40 iron and select the starting Depot; click Build Depot then click empty land entirely within the ring. Invalid land or outside territory leaves balances unchanged; Esc cancels placement. Click the new Depot to inspect its empty independent inventory. In overlap, select the poor Depot and verify rich neighbours cannot fund it. For repeated experiments, set Depot instance Initial Energy / Initial Iron in Inspector before running; -1 uses rule defaults.

Restart, gather 30 iron, select starting Depot and print a Depot Truck. Select the stretched placeholder after its queue completes, move to open land, and deploy. Inspect its 100 energy / 0 iron and independent territory. Invalid or occupied footprints retain the truck. To test capture, author another Depot instance near units with Team enemy or neutral; move infantry within 12 units, keep enemy units outside, wait 8 seconds. Add an opposing unit within 12 to verify contest resets. Verify stored inventory survives capture and paid queues cancel without refund. Enemy takeover of selected Depot hides its ring and disables spending.

Edit depot.tscn for model slot, footprint, queue and print duration; depot_truck.tscn for placeholder/model and movement; depot_rules.tres for shared economy/capture defaults; individual Depot instances for ownership and initial inventories. Runtime stock, capture and queues live on instances, never shared resources. Terrain, lights and visual meshes remain authored scenes.
